# context/

Background for Erosion.exe, gathered from the design conversation where the concept, the look and the crawlers were worked out. Nothing here goes live (GitHub Pages only publishes `site/`).

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `PROJECT_BRIEF.md` | Full report: concept, rules of the space, look, crawler behaviour and technique, decisions, planned steps, parked ideas, open questions. Read this first. |
| `reference/mockups/` | Image mockups of the look: home, a room, mobile, five hypothetical pages, and `erosion_random_piece.png` (an invented piece with broken crops and scale; source in `random_piece.html`). |
| `reference/sitemap/` | `erosion_sitemap.png` and `sitemap.svg`: a hypothetical map of pages and links, including crawler spread. Inspiration only. |
| `reference/crawler-demo/` | `erosion_crawlers_live.gif` and a still: the live crawl_33/38/39 demo after the heads were removed. |
| `reference/crawler-sheets/` | Crawler design sheets crawl_1 to crawl_40 (PNG plus the HTML/SVG sources). |
| `demos/demo-01-figure/` | The 12-dot figure: tap a dot to wake it, drag to lead it, PURGE to reset. |
| `demos/demo-02-crawlers-33-38-39/` | Live glyph crawlers crawl_33 ribs, crawl_38 octobrace, crawl_39 feather. |

The demos are standalone. Open their `index.html` by double-clicking. Copy code from them into an experiment folder (`site/erosion/<name>/`) rather than linking to them.

Priority if anything conflicts: repo `CLAUDE.md`, then `docs/EROSION_DESIGN.md`, then this folder.
