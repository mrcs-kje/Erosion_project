# Live crawlers: crawl_33 ribs, crawl_38 octobrace, crawl_39 feather

Open `index.html` (double-click, no build step). Pointer near a creature makes its limbs lean toward it. A tap or click makes it react.

- crawl_33: a wave runs down the spine, ribs curl tighter on tap.
- crawl_38: eight bracket-character arms ripple and splay on tap.
- crawl_39: barbs flutter along the spine and fan open on tap.

Heads, the curly-brace body of crawl_38 and the antennae of crawl_39 were removed on 29 Oct. All the drawing is in `js/crawlers.js`; numbers marked `DIAL` are safe to change. `erosion_crawlers_live.gif` is a short recording.
