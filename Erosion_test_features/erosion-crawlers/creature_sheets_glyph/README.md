# Creature sheets crawl_1 to crawl_40

Four design sheets of crawler ideas, ten per sheet, drawn in the Erosion look (orange lines, dots and characters on near-black).

| Sheet | Creatures | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| `erosion_crawlers_sheet.png` | crawl_1 to crawl_10 | first round of simple forms |
| `erosion_crawlers_sheet2.png` | crawl_11 to crawl_20 | more complex, out of the box |
| `erosion_crawlers_sheet3.png` | crawl_21 to crawl_30 | glyph / typographic creatures |
| `erosion_crawlers_sheet4.png` | crawl_31 to crawl_40 | variations on crawl_26 with tendrils |

`sheet_sources/` holds the HTML/SVG that draws each sheet (open in a browser). These sheets are the original designs, with heads. The live demo of crawl_33, 38 and 39 (heads removed) is in `../creature_live_glyph_33_38_39/`.
