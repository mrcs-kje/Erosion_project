# Erosion.exe: ideas

One running list of every idea for the project, big or small, built or not. Add new ones at the bottom of the right section. Nothing here is a decision. Ideas can combine, mutate or be dropped.

Status: `open` (just an idea) / `wip` (being worked on) / `parked` (not now) / `built` (in the site or a demo).
Bigger material (videos, screenshots, code references) can still get its own file in `ideas/`, named `YYYY-MM-DD_short-name.md`, with a line here pointing to it.

## Concept and behaviour

- `open` **Missed erosion.** Remember when a guest was last here and apply all the erosion they missed in one go on their next visit. A cheap way to make "neglect wears" real. (manifesto, 2026-10-30)
- `open` **Guests mutate things.** Guests can add, "delete" and mutate things, not just watch. Stored only in their own browser for now. (manifesto, 2026-10-30)
- `parked` **Shared changes.** Guests' changes visible to every visitor. Needs a server, far future. (manifesto, 2026-10-30)
- `open` **Index only sometimes.** The Index is not always reachable. It opens on some days or some visits. (manifesto, 2026-10-30)
- `open` **Dead links as skeletons.** Broken links stay and read as remains of something that once took form and changed. (manifesto, 2026-10-30)
- `open` **Broken threshold.** The way into Erosion.exe is a deliberately broken passage: odd graphics, wrong crops, wrong scale. (project brief)
- `open` **Other artists.** Later, other artists upload, visually anonymous (no bylines). (project brief)
- `open` **Domain.** Something like erosionexe.xyz. Relative paths only until then. (project brief)
- `parked` **Browser extension / desktop overlay.** Erosion leaking onto other sites or the desktop. Not as pets; only if it fits erosion. (project brief)

## Manifesto

- `open` **Manifesto fractures.** Lines of the manifesto scattered across the site as fragments. A guest collects them and reads what they found on a more central page (e.g. the Index). (2026-10-30)
- `open` **Eroding manifesto.** The manifesto erodes while it is read: words go missing or get eaten. (2026-10-30)

## Creatures

- `built` **Build and repair.** Creatures construct, not only eat. They stand for the site and the code itself. Demo 15: strands between words that grow into scaffolds, other creatures cross them as bridges, a strand snaps when its word is eaten. (manifesto, 2026-10-30)
- `built` **Mending dead links (rare).** A creature weaves threads up to a struck-through header link; it lives for 1 to 2 minutes, leads to another room, then dies again. (demo 15, 2026-10-30)
- `open` **Mending scars.** Creatures write an eaten word back into its gap, letter by letter, sometimes slightly wrong, so the text mutates. (creatures thread, 2026-10-30, not picked yet)
- `open` **Patching each other.** A fond creature regrows a lost limb on a hurt one. (creatures thread, 2026-10-30, not picked yet)
- `built` **Growing without a pupa.** Creatures that only grow longer (glyph crawlers, siphon colonies, INKLING, LEVIATHAN) grow into their next stage as they eat; only real changes of shape go through a pupa. (demo 16, 2026-10-30)
- `built` **Snake grows instead of evolving.** One segment per word, bigger mandibles with food and age, lays small snake eggs at full length. (demo 14, 2026-10-30)
- `built` **Squid family on its own.** Only as a random hatching. (demo 14, 2026-10-30; the line itself was replaced in demo 17)
- `built` **Siphon line.** Colony creature after siphonophore and hydra references, grows by budding. (demo 13, 2026-10-29)
- `built` **Feeding by hand.** Hold a word to pull it out of the text and give it to a creature, or drop it as food. (demo 13, 2026-10-29)
- `open` **Branching evolution.** One form can evolve into 2+ different forms, by chance. (Figma creature page, 2026-10-30)
- `open` **Real designs.** Replace stand-in forms with the user's own creature designs over time. (2026-10-29)
- `built` **Squid line redesign.** New family made of characters: SPECK > SQUIDLET > SQUID > GIANT > KRAKEN. Starts as a speck, ends very, very big, grows the whole way (no pupa), a kraken bursts into specks. (demo 17, 2026-10-30)
- `parked` **OLD ONE and LEVIATHAN.** Out for now, they don't fit the atmosphere. Code kept. (2026-10-30)
- `open` **Sea angel.** A small drifting creature of characters with two flapping wing lobes, after Clione. (owner's marine photos, 2026-10-30)
- `open` **Fan-fin larva.** A fish larva with big fanned fins of spread characters that open and close as it swims, after flying-fish larvae. (owner's marine photos, 2026-10-30)
- `open` **Spiky larva.** A larva with fans of spines (| / and \ lines) that it raises when threatened. (owner's marine photos, 2026-10-30)
- `open` **Paddle worm.** A long worm of segments, each with a pair of paddles that row in a wave down the body, after a polychaete. (owner's marine photos, 2026-10-30)
- `open` **Shared point pool.** Creatures swap and merge through a shared pool of points instead of fixed bodies. (project brief)

## Spots and pages

- `wip` **Unspecified Game.** Endless ASCII Canvas navigation game in phases with different mechanics (isometric movement, choose-your-own-adventure with portraits). Esoteric and confusing. (2026-10-30)
  - Known so far: world made only of ASCII characters, animated by symbols shifting. Endless, changes each visit. Own new room (site/erosion/game-unspecified/). Needs a Figma page. Thread "Unspecified Game". (2026-10-30)
  - Decided: the player is a cursor/process that changes form in each phase. Phases are tied together by hidden clues only attentive players notice. (2026-10-30)
  - `open` Phase: maze whose walls are text that rewrites itself. (suggested, 2026-10-30)
  - `open` Phase: terminal where you type commands to move. (suggested, 2026-10-30)
  - `wip` Phase: dark screen, you only see a small radius around you, but some things exist and show beyond it. (picked, 2026-10-30)
  - `open` Phase: fake crash/BIOS screen you can walk around in. (suggested, 2026-10-30)
  - `wip` Leaving a phase: find an exit symbol, or crash and wake up in the next one. (picked, 2026-10-30)
  - `open` Leaving a phase by the phase eroding around you. (suggested, not picked, 2026-10-30)
  - `wip` Losing leaves traces of you for the next visit. (picked, 2026-10-30)
  - Decided: hidden clues lead nowhere final, just more strangeness. Beyond your sight radius in the dark: something that moves, plus your own traces. Traces are both your own earlier runs (saved in your browser) and fake "other visitors" the game invents. First prototype: isometric movement. Controls: keyboard and click/tap, optimized for phones. (2026-10-30)
- `wip` **Drawing dump.** Endless canvas you pan around, order randomized each visit, new drawings incl. self-drawing / glitching ones. Secret, off the Index. (2026-10-30)

- `built` **Loop: eye and crack.** The red door at the end of the loop corridor is now a red eye that blinks and follows the pointer. The player has a crack in its frame: hold or drag it and it widens until the player breaks in two and falls away, showing the page behind. (2026-10-30)

## Possible combinations

Ideas from above that could merge into one.

- **Creatures carry fractures.** Creatures pick up manifesto fragments and drop them on other pages, so finding them means following what moves.
- **Repair while you're away.** Missed erosion + build/repair: on your next visit, things have both worn down and been rebuilt by the creatures, as visible new layers.
- **Building on skeletons.** Creatures build on dead links, so a skeleton slowly grows into something new (deletion = mutation). (A first, temporary version is the mending in demo 15.)
- **Fractures in the game.** Manifesto fragments can also be found inside the Unspecified Game, as symbols you walk over.
